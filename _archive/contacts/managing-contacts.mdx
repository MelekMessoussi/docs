Your contacts are the foundation of every Myna campaign. Each contact represents a person your AI agent will reach out to — and every piece of information you store on that contact helps the agent personalize its conversations and track progress toward your goals.

## Core Contact Fields

Every contact in Myna has a set of standard fields. The table below describes what each field captures and how it is used.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **First Name / Last Name** | The contact's full name, displayed throughout the dashboard and used in personalized messages. |
| **Phone Number** | The primary identifier for a contact. Phone numbers must be unique within your workspace. |
| **Email** | Optional email address for the contact. |
| **Address** | Full or structured address (street, city, state, zip, country). |
| **Contact Type** | Classifies the contact as a **Lead**, **Customer**, or **Employee**. |
| **Status** | Tracks where this contact is in the campaign lifecycle (e.g., Unassigned, Preparing Campaign, In Conversation, Won, Cold). |
| **Lead Source** | Where this contact came from (e.g., referral, web form, GoHighLevel import). |
| **Campaign** | The active campaign this contact is enrolled in. |
| **Channel** | The communication channel used — defaults to SMS. |
| **Lead Cost** | Optional cost associated with acquiring this lead. |
| **Deal Value** | Optional estimated deal or contract value. |
| **Secondary Contact** | A spouse, partner, or other secondary person associated with this contact, including their name, phone, and relationship. |
| **Notes** | Free-text notes visible to your team. |
| **Custom Fields** | Any additional fields you've created in Settings → Custom Fields. |

## Adding a Contact Manually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Navigate to **Contacts** in the left sidebar and click **Add Contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in contact details">
    Enter the contact's first and last name, phone number (required), and any other fields that apply — email, address, lead source, and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a contact type and campaign">
    Set the **Contact Type** (Lead, Customer, or Employee) and optionally assign the contact to an existing **Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tags (optional)">
    Select one or more tags to segment this contact. You can create new tags inline if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the contact">
    Click **Save**. The contact is immediately available in your contact list and ready to be enrolled in a campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Importing Contacts via CSV

Use CSV import to add contacts in bulk. The import process accepts CSV or Excel files and supports up to 2,000 contacts per file.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your spreadsheet">
    Create a CSV or Excel file with at least a `phone` column. Optional columns include `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `address`, `street_address`, `city`, `state`, `zip_code`, `country`, and `notes`. Column headers must match these names exactly.

    ```csv
    first_name,last_name,phone,email,lead_source
    Jane,Smith,+15555550101,jane@example.com,Web Form
    Carlos,Reyes,+15555550202,,Referral
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    Go to **Contacts** and click **Import Contacts** (or the upload icon). Select your CSV or Excel file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set import options">
    Choose a **Lead Source** to apply to all imported contacts. Optionally, select a **Campaign** to enroll them in immediately and any **Tags** to attach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Myna previews the import. Contacts with duplicate phone numbers are skipped. Click **Import** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    After the import completes, a summary shows how many contacts were created, skipped (duplicates), and any rows that had errors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Contacts imported without a campaign assignment will be set to **Unassigned** status. You can enroll them in a campaign from the contact list or the contact detail page at any time.
</Note>

## Syncing Contacts from GoHighLevel

If your workspace is connected to GoHighLevel, you can import contacts directly from your GHL sub-account. See the [GoHighLevel integration guide](/integrations/gohighlevel) for step-by-step instructions on connecting and running a contact sync.

## Managing a Contact

Click any contact in the list to open the contact detail view. From here you can:

- **Edit contact fields** — Update name, email, address, deal value, or any custom field.
- **Change campaign status** — Move the contact to a different status stage or reassign them to a different campaign.
- **View message history** — See the full conversation thread between the AI agent and this contact.
- **Add notes** — Leave internal notes for your team that are not sent to the contact.
- **Attach files** — Upload documents or images associated with this contact.
- **Start a campaign** — Enroll this contact in a campaign directly from the detail page by assigning them to the campaign and setting their status to *Preparing Campaign*.

## User Takeover

When you need to step into a conversation personally, toggle **User Takeover** on the contact. This pauses all AI responses for that contact so you can reply manually.

<Note>
  While User Takeover is active, Myna will not send any automated messages to the contact — the AI is completely paused. Toggle it off when you are done to resume normal AI-driven messaging.
</Note>

You can also enable User Takeover from the Conversations view without navigating to the contact detail page.

## Starring a Contact

Click the star icon on any contact to mark it as starred. Starred contacts appear in the **Starred** filter in the Conversations view, making it easy to find your most important leads at a glance. Click the star again to remove it.

## Viewing Contacts in Kanban Mode

Switch between **List** and **Kanban** views using the toggle in the top-right of the Contacts page. Kanban mode groups contacts by status so you can see your pipeline at a glance and track how leads are moving through each stage.

<Tip>
  Use the **Search** bar to quickly find a contact by name, phone number, or email — especially useful when your contact list grows large.
</Tip>
