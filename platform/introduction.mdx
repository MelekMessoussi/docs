In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations with clients and reliable follow-through. However, achieving both has long been a time consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna is a no-code platform that lets anyone build AI agents. A Myna agent talks with your customers and leads over SMS, WhatsApp, webchat and email, and it can act on what they say. It checks your calendar and books appointments, updates contact records, opens support tickets and passes conversations to your team when needed. You decide what each agent does, what it knows and how it speaks, and Myna runs it around the clock.
