## **What is Myna?**

In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations and reliable follow-through. However, achieving both has long been a time-consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna is an easy-to-use agent-building platform that brings together intelligent conversations and automated task handling in one unified workspace. Whether you run a small clinic or manage dozens of client accounts at an agency, you can set up a personalized agent that fits how your business works, without writing a single line of code.

Your agent works on the channels your customers already use, including SMS, WhatsApp, webchat and email. It answers their questions, collects all the details you need and takes care of the work that follows, such as booking appointments in your calendar, updating contact records and opening support tickets. It only confirms an action to a customer once it is done, so what your customers are told is always accurate. If a request calls for a human, it passes the conversation to your team with the context already in place.

You stay in control of what your agent can access and do, from the tools it uses to the contact fields it can read or edit. Once it’s set up, Myna keeps it running at any hour.
