In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations and reliable follow through. However, achieving both has long been a time consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna is an easy-to-use agent-building platform that brings together intelligent conversations and automated task handling in one unified workspace. Whether you run a small clinic or manage dozens of client accounts at an agency, you can build and set up an agent that fits how your business works.

With Myna, every conversation can lead straight to a result: Our AI agents reply to customers on the channels they already use, including SMS, WhatsApp, webchat and email, and they complete the tasks those conversations call for. They book appointments in your calendar, keep contact records up to date and open support tickets, and they only confirm an action to a customer once it is actually done.

Its agents answer questions, collect the details you need and take care of routine work like booking appointments, updating contact records and opening support tickets..
