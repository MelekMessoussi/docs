In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations and reliable follow-through. However, achieving both has long been a time consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna replaces that patchwork with AI agents you can build yourself, without any technical background. Each agent replies to customers and leads on SMS, WhatsApp, webchat and email, and it follows through on what it promises. It books appointments straight into your calendar, updates contact records and opens support tickets, and it knows when to hand a conversation to someone on your team. You shape how it behaves and what it knows, and it never takes a day off.
