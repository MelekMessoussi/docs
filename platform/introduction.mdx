In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations and reliable follow through. However, achieving both has long been a time consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna is an easy-to-use agent-building platform that brings together intelligent conversations and automated task handling in one unified workspace. Whether you run a small clinic or manage dozens of client accounts at an agency, you can set up a personalized agent that fits how your business works, without writing a single line of code and teams that want finer control can set tool permissions and decide which contact fields an agent can read or edit..

With Myna, your agent works on the channels your customers already use, including SMS, WhatsApp, webchat and email. It answers their questions, collects the details you need and takes care of the work that follows, such as booking appointments in your calendar, updating contact records and opening support tickets. If a request calls for a human, it passes the conversation to your team with the context already in place.

You stay in control of what each agent does, while Myna keeps it available at any hour.
