In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations and reliable follow through. However, achieving both has long been a time consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna is a no-code platform that brings both pillars into one place. It lets you build AI agents that talk with your customers and leads over SMS, WhatsApp, webchat and email, then carry out what those conversations call for.

Myna is a no-code platform for building AI agents that talk to your customers and take care of what they ask for. It is built for businesses that handle a high volume of messages and need every one of them answered and acted on: Whether you run a small clinic or manage dozens of client accounts at an agency, you can set up an agent that fits how your business works, without any technical background.
