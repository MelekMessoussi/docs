In customer experience, the path to success is built on two essential pillars: fast, personal conversations and reliable follow through. However, achieving both has long been a time consuming challenge, requiring large teams, long hours and a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Myna is an easy-to-use, no-code platform that brings together intelligent agentic conversations and automated task handling in one unified workspace.

 Its a no-code platform for building AI agents that talk to your customers and take care of what they ask for. It is built for businesses that handle a high volume of messages and need every one of them answered and acted on: Whether you run a small clinic or manage dozens of client accounts at an agency, you can set up an agent that fits how your business works, without any technical background.
